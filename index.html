Sanitized case studies

Problem → action → result.

High-denial group practice

Problem: Eligibility, authorization, coding and submission errors pushed denials above 17%.

Action: Root-cause categories, claim scrubbing, QA, SOP updates and weekly denial review.

Result: Denials reduced to 6% or less; first-pass claims exceeded 98%.

Telehealth modifier/POS errors

Problem: Virtual claims denied because modifier and POS combinations did not meet payer rules.

Action: Payer-specific mapping, corrected claims, training and pre-submission validation.

Result: Claims recovered and repeat errors reduced.

Invalid primary diagnosis

Problem: Aged claims remained unpaid because diagnoses were unsupported or sequenced incorrectly.

Action: Documentation, payer edits, sequencing and authorization review.

Result: Aged balances cleared and future recurrence prevented.

Missing claims

Problem: Completed encounters were not transmitted for billing.

Action: Reconciled schedule, attendance, notes, charges and clearinghouse acknowledgment.

Result: Missing claims billed and practice collections increased.
